# µLearn — Website Knowledge Base
> Source: https://mulearn.org
> Last updated: 2024-11-05

## What is µLearn?
µLearn is a community-driven education-initiation platform backed by GTech (Government of Kerala's technology initiative) and IEEE. It transforms traditional learning through peer-to-peer collaboration, gamification, and real-world skill development. Rather than top-down lectures, µLearn promotes a synergistic philosophy of education where students learn by doing, collaborating, and teaching each other. The platform bridges the gap between formal education and industry needs by connecting learners with mentors, industry partners, hackathons, and companies.

## Interest Groups (IGs)
Interest Groups are domain-specific communities within µLearn where learners who share a passion for a particular technology or field come together to learn collaboratively. IGs break down silos by connecting peers and mentors across different colleges and disciplines.

Popular Interest Groups include:
- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML)
- Web Development
- Mobile App Development
- UI/UX & Creative Design
- Cyber Security
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Robotics & Digital Fabrication
- AR/VR/XR (Extended Reality)
- Cloud Computing
- Data Science
- Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Within these groups, members attend office hours, tackle guided challenges, share resources, and review each other's projects. Each IG is led by an Interest Group Lead who organizes activities and mentors members.

## Karma Points
Karma points are the gamification backbone of the µLearn platform. They are visible proof of a learner's consistency, practical effort, and active contribution to the community. Unlike traditional grades, Karma points are earned through real-world actions:

- Completing coding tasks, design assignments, or mini-projects
- Participating in community challenges and tech bootcamps
- Helping peers, organizing learning circles, or contributing to open source
- Submitting proof of work (code repositories, design files, write-ups) reviewed by peers or mentors
- Attending and contributing to events and workshops

Karma points drive leaderboards where students and colleges compete. They reflect directly on your µID profile, and potential employers look at domain-specific Karma points to gauge practical capability.

## Campus Chapters
Campus chapters are college-level µLearn communities. Each campus chapter operates autonomously under the µLearn umbrella, organizing local events, learning circles, and Interest Group activities. Campus chapters have their own leads, coordinators, and community managers.

µLearn CHN is the campus chapter at the College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC). It is part of the broader GTech µLearn network spanning colleges across Kerala and beyond.

## Learning Circles
Learning Circles are small peer groups (typically 3-7 members) within µLearn who study, build projects, and solve real-world challenges together. They are the fundamental unit of collaborative learning in µLearn. Each learning circle focuses on a specific topic or skill area and meets regularly to share progress, review each other's work, and tackle challenges.

## µID (Digital Profile)
Every µLearn user gets a permanent digital profile called a µID. Unlike a static resume, a µID is a verifiable timeline of your actual work, contributions, skills, and activities. It tracks:
- Karma points earned across different domains
- Projects completed and proof of work submitted
- Events attended and contributions made
- Interest Groups participated in
- Learning Circles joined

Your µID serves as a living portfolio that employers and recruiters can review to gauge your practical capabilities.

## How to Join µLearn
1. Visit https://mulearn.org and click "Join" or "Get Started"
2. Create your account to receive your µID
3. Join Interest Groups that match your passions
4. Find or create a Learning Circle at your campus
5. Start completing tasks, earning Karma points, and building your profile

If your college has a campus chapter (like µLearn CHN at CEC), connect with your campus lead for local activities and events.
