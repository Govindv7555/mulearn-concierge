## What is µLearn?
µLearn (also written "Mu Learn") is a peer-learning platform and community launched by GTech, the Group of Technology Companies, which is the industry body of IT companies in Kerala. It is described as an industry-enabled digital platform for peer learning. Its philosophy is "a synergic philosophy of education, with a culture of mutual learning through micro peer groups": instead of top-down lectures, students learn by doing, collaborating, and teaching each other. The platform aims to close the gap between industry and academia by connecting learners with mentors, industry partners, challenges, and events.

Website: https://mulearn.org

## Interest Groups (IGs)
Interest Groups are domain-specific communities within µLearn where learners who share an interest in a technology or field learn together. They connect peers and mentors across different colleges. Examples of areas an Interest Group can cover include AI and machine learning, web development, mobile apps, design, cyber security, IoT, cloud computing, and data science.

Within these groups, members take on guided challenges, share resources, and review each other's projects. Each Interest Group is led by an Interest Group Lead who organises activities and mentors members.

## Karma Points
Karma points are µLearn's way of recognising contributions. They are awarded for actively contributing and for reaching milestones, for example by completing tasks and challenges, taking part in events, helping peers, and submitting work for review. Karma points show a learner's consistency and practical effort, and they appear on the learner's µLearn profile and in community leaderboards.

## Campus Chapters
Campus chapters are college-level µLearn communities that run local events, learning circles, and Interest Group activities, with their own leads and coordinators.

µLearn CHN is the campus chapter at the College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC), part of the wider GTech µLearn network of colleges.

## Learning Circles
Learning Circles are small peer groups within µLearn that study a topic or skill, build projects, and solve challenges together. They follow the "micro peer groups" idea at the heart of µLearn: each circle meets regularly to share progress and review each other's work.

## µID (Digital Profile)
Every µLearn member has a µLearn profile (µID) that records their activity on the platform, such as Karma points, completed tasks, events attended, and the Interest Groups and Learning Circles they take part in. It works as a living portfolio of practical work rather than a static resume.

## How to Join µLearn
1. Visit https://mulearn.org and sign up to get your µLearn profile (µID).
2. Join the µLearn Discord server to meet the community: https://discord.gg/5xdYHfjFt
3. Join an Interest Group that matches your interests.
4. Find or start a Learning Circle with peers at your campus.
5. Start completing tasks, earning Karma points, and building your profile.

If your college has a campus chapter (like µLearn CHN at CEC), connect with your campus lead for local activities and events.

## Events and Programs
µLearn organises programs and events such as hackathons, tech bootcamps and workshops, community challenges, orientation sessions, and industry mentorship sessions. Dates and details change often, so check https://mulearn.org and the µLearn Discord #announcements channel for the latest.

## µLearn Discord Community
The µLearn Discord server is the main real-time hub of the community. It carries announcements about events, programs, and opportunities, plus Interest Group channels, help channels, and community spaces.
