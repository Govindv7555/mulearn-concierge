# AI Interest Group: First 3 Months
**µLearn CHN · Govind Vijay**

**Goal:** In 12 weeks, move members from "curious about AI" to "has shipped something with AI", and leave a group that can run without me.

**What I bring:** I build to deadlines. I worked in a 32-hour hackathon team (RentHub) with a frontend/backend split agreed through an API contract, and I built this concierge end to end, from retrieval to deployment. I will run the group the same way: small teams, clear contracts, and something working at every checkpoint.

## Month 1: Start and get everyone to a first win
- **Week 1: Kick-off.** Ice-breaker, a 15-minute live demo of this chatbot, and a short survey: current level, interests, free hours. Members are sorted into mixed-level pods of 4–5.
- **Weeks 2–4: Weekly hands-on session (90 min).** 20 minutes of talk, 70 minutes of doing. Topics: how LLMs work, prompting well, calling an AI API.
- **Mini project (week 4):** every member ships a tiny AI app, such as a summariser or a Q&A bot over their own notes, and shares it in 2 minutes.

## Month 2: Build in pods
- **Sessions:** retrieval and RAG, evaluating answers, and responsible AI (privacy, hallucinations, where free models log data).
- **Pod projects:** each pod picks one real problem for campus or µLearn, e.g. a timetable assistant, a lab-notes search, or a Karma-task helper. Weekly 15-minute stand-ups and a shared board.
- **Guest slot:** one senior or alumnus talk, or a recorded industry session.

## Month 3: Ship and share
- **Weeks 9–11:** polish, test, document. Every project needs a README, a demo link and a short write-up.
- **Week 12: Demo day.** Pods present to the campus; the best projects go to µLearn for Karma and visibility.
- **Handover:** document the playbook and train two members to lead the next cycle.

## Engaging members
- Pods, so nobody is alone. A weekly "show something you made" slot, however small.
- A Discord channel with a **weekly challenge** and a public shout-out for progress, not only for winners.
- Low-pressure entry: beginner tasks first, no prior AI knowledge needed, and pairing for anyone stuck.

## Mentoring
- Each pod has a lead and a "buddy" from the more experienced members, with a 10-minute check-in each week.
- I do short 1:1s with quiet or struggling members at the end of months 1 and 2.
- Code reviews are friendly and specific, and review work counts as contribution.

## How I will know it worked
| Measure | Target by week 12 |
|---|---|
| Members who attend at least 6 of 10 sessions | 60% |
| Members who ship a working project | 70% |
| Pod projects demoed publicly | 4 or more |
| Members who volunteer to lead or mentor next | 2 or more |
| Satisfaction in a short end-of-month survey | 4 out of 5 |

**Risks and answers:** low attendance leads to shorter sessions at a fixed weekly slot and recordings; mixed skill levels lead to pods with tiered tasks; tool or quota limits lead to free tiers and shared keys kept out of the repo.
